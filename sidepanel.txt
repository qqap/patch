how to use the sidepanel, you ask?

it's simple. make sure you click "show reading list", and the sidepanel for reading list appears.
in settings, change the sidepanel location to left or right, depending on your preference. i do left.

features:
- to close a tab, use the command ctrl-w
- tabs reorder automagically based on recency.
- resize the sidepanel, to your liking.
- also, just use a single window.
- omnibox in middle (on a 14-inch mac), otherwise it's offset randomly
- fit like 34 tabs on a 14 inch screen.
- omnibox also blurred transparent, which looks nice
- other things that i dont remember
