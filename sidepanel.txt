if apple says you are not allowed to open the app, or it's damaged, just do
xattr -l /path/to/application.app
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /path/to/application.app
and other attributes you see.

it's simple. make sure you click "show reading list", and the sidepanel for reading list appears.
in settings, change the sidepanel location to left since right is borked.

features:
- to close a tab, use the command ctrl-w
- tabs reorder automagically based on recency.
- resize the sidepanel, to your liking.
- also, just use a single window.
- omnibox in middle (on a 14-inch mac), otherwise it's offset randomly
- fit like 34 tabs on a 14 inch screen.
- omnibox also blurred transparent, which looks nice
- picture in picture, looks rounded with shadow, better
- customize color with chrome://customize-chrome-side-panel.top-chrome
- new tab page actually blank
- use youtube-music instead of spotify. torrent instead of netflix.
- other things that i dont remember
